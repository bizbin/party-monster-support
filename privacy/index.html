Privacy policy

Your party stays on your phone.

Effective October 9, 2026 · Party Monster for iPhone

Party Monster does not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal data with its developer. The app has no ads, tracking, analytics, developer crash reporting, account system, or cloud sync.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores recent player names, player appearance preferences, app settings, selected photo avatars, saved creative game props, local photo-trial status, and verified purchase access information on your iPhone. Secret words, answers, votes, and motion samples are not uploaded or retained as a permanent game history.

Camera and selected photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker gives the app access to pictures you select. Photo avatars and creative cutouts are processed on your iPhone using Apple’s image-processing features. Images are not uploaded and are not automatically added to your Photos library.

Motion

Balance Ball uses your iPhone’s motion sensors during active play to move the ball. Motion input stops when you pause or leave the game. Motion samples are not sent to the developer or stored as a history.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and payments through StoreKit under Apple’s policies. The developer does not receive your payment-card information. The app checks Apple’s verified purchase information to provide and restore paid access. Purchases, restoration, and account changes may require a network connection.

Apple’s privacy policy explains how Apple handles information used by its services.

Deleting local information

In the app, use Settings → Forget recent players and photos to clear saved players and photo avatars. Use Settings → Manage saved photos to remove creative game props. You can manage saved props without paid creative access. Deleting the app removes its local app data; paid purchases can be restored through your Apple Account.

Children and personal data

The app does not request account registration or collect personal data from children. Camera use and purchases are optional. The app’s App Store age rating describes its content; parents can manage purchases and permissions using Apple’s parental controls.

Support messages

If you email support, the information you choose to include in your message is used to answer your request and resolve the issue. Support correspondence is separate from app use and is handled by the support email service. Do not send photos or private player information unless needed for your request.

This website

These support pages do not use analytics, advertising cookies, forms, or a login. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to serve and protect the website.

Policy updates

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
