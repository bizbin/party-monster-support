Help & support

Keep the party going.

Help with games, purchases, and photos in Party Monster.

Contact supportfeeyarcat@gmail.com

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending photos or private player information.

Starting a game

Choose a game, add the required players, check the options and rules, and start the round. Six classic games are free and can be replayed without a purchase. Classic Pack unlocks the remaining ten games in this 16-game collection.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings and choose Restore purchases, or use Restore purchases on the pack screen. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoration may need an internet connection. Photos and photo-trial history stay on the original iPhone and are not restored with purchases.

Classic and Complete

Classic Pack includes this 16-game collection. Complete Pack adds player photo avatars and the supported Creative Mode features in Balance Ball and Spin the Bottle. Classic Pack owners can buy the separate Complete Upgrade. These are one-time purchases with no subscription or automatic recurring charge.

Complete also includes future official local Creative Mode updates for this collection. New collections and services may be sold separately. Creative features vary by game and iPhone capability.

Photos and permissions

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. You can also choose a picture with the system photo picker. If camera access was denied, change it in iPhone Settings for Party Monster, or use the photo picker instead.

Cutouts are processed on your iPhone. If a photo cannot be processed, try another picture with a clearer subject, or choose the available original-photo fallback. Classic play and monster avatars remain available.

Deleting saved photos

Use Settings → Forget recent players and photos to clear recent players and their photo avatars. Game props are stored separately under Settings → Manage saved photos. You can manage or delete saved game props without a paid creative entitlement.

Offline play

Games, artwork, and question packs are included in the app. Purchasing and restoring paid access use Apple’s services and may need a connection. Player names and photos stay on the iPhone; there is no account system or cloud sync.

Playing safely

Party Monster requires iOS 17 or later. During motion games, sit securely, keep a firm hold on your iPhone, and never throw it. Pause or leave the game to stop motion input.
